2001 BMW 740iL (E38)

Full Mechanical Restoration · Winter 2021 – Present

Engine Swap Transmission Rebuild BMW INPA Cam Timing Correction

Overview

Purchased as a running vehicle with heavy exhaust smoke, this V8-powered luxury sedan became a full mechanical restoration. The project involved diagnosing complex multi-system failures — including a mistimed replacement engine and a failed transmission install — and resolving each through systematic root cause analysis and hands-on repair.

Photos

Major Work Performed

  • Initial diagnostics and cooling system repair (coolant pipe replacement)
  • PCV system service and oil system evaluation
  • Full engine removal and replacement with sourced M62 unit
  • Transmission replacement after improper torque converter installation
  • BMW INPA diagnostics to isolate misfires to one cylinder bank
  • Camshaft timing correction using timing kit and TDC alignment procedure
  • Restored proper firing across all cylinders after timing correction
  • Identified and replaced cracked transmission valve body using known-good unit to resolve rough shifting
  • Catalytic converter and muffler modification for improved exhaust flow
  • Front bumper sourcing and installation with passenger-side mount straightening
  • Driver-side fender panel sourcing and installation
  • H&R lowering spring installation

2004 Lexus GX470 (UZJ120)

Structural Fabrication, Brakes & Engine Maintenance · 2024 – Present

Structural Welding Metal Fabrication Rust Remediation Chassis Repair Timing Belt Service

Overview

Successfully restored the structural integrity of a Lexus GX470 by remediating extensive frame corrosion. This project involved high-precision metal fabrication, structural welding, and chemical treatment to halt oxidation and ensure long-term safety and durability of the load-bearing chassis structure.

Work Performed

  • Rust removal using pneumatic needle scaler on accessible frame areas
  • Cut out severely corroded sections and fabricated custom metal replacement panels
  • Repaired and precisely realigned broken rear trailing arm mount
  • Applied phosphoric acid solution to convert remaining rust into a stable surface, followed by protective paint
  • Replaced corroded brake hard lines from under driver's seat to rear calipers
  • Replaced rear sway bar links including one fully rusted-off link
  • Performed timing belt service using an Aisin timing belt kit, replacing the timing belt, water pump, tensioner, and idler pulley
  • Rebuilt the front passenger-side brake caliper, replaced the front driver-side caliper, and installed new brake pads
  • Replaced corroded brake lines and bled the hydraulic brake system
  • Applied a full Fluid Film undercoating to help protect the chassis and underbody from further corrosion

Timing Belt & Cooling System Service

Serviced the 4.7L V8 timing belt drive using an Aisin kit, including a replacement water pump, tensioner and idler pulley. The work involved exposing the front of the engine, aligning timing references and reassembling the belt drive with fresh components.

Brake System & Corrosion Protection

Rebuilt the front right brake caliper, replaced the front left caliper, fitted new pads and repaired corroded hydraulic lines. After the frame repairs and rust treatment, applied Fluid Film undercoating across the underbody for additional corrosion protection.

2006 Range Rover Supercharged (L322)

Completed Automotive Project · Fall 2025 – 2026

Sold for $4,000 Electrical Diagnostics Paint & Clear-Coat Repair Transmission Service

Overview

Purchased a non-starting 2006 Range Rover Supercharged for $500 and carried out electrical troubleshooting, mechanical servicing, cosmetic repairs, and paint refinishing. With approximately $500 spent on parts and supplies, the vehicle was ultimately sold for $4,000. This project combined hands-on diagnostics with practical cost management and a new set of paint-finishing skills.

Diagnosis & Repairs

  • Investigated a no-crank condition related to the transmission park/neutral starting circuit and restored starting functionality
  • Replaced the battery and starter and addressed a sticking shifter mechanism
  • Serviced the transmission, including fluid, filter and solenoid work
  • Converted the suspension to a conventional spring setup
  • Refurbished the headlights with replacement lenses and resealed the housings using butyl sealant

Hood Paint & Finish Work

The hood refinishing was one of the most time-intensive parts of the project and an opportunity to learn aerosol paint repair. I practiced surface preparation, applying paint and clear coat, blending the new finish into the existing coating, and sanding and buffing to improve the final appearance. The process involved experimentation and correction rather than simply replacing a panel.
