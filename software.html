Pull List — Salvage Yard Inventory Explorer

A mobile-friendly application designed to make sourcing automotive parts at Chicago-area salvage yards more efficient. The site organizes vehicle inventory and parts opportunities to help prioritize which cars and components to inspect.

JavaScriptPythonGitHub PagesInventory dataMobile UIAutomation

Problem

Finding worthwhile parts across multiple salvage-yard inventories takes time. Vehicle generation differences, part compatibility and row locations can make the process harder.

Approach

  • Combine inventory information from multiple Chicago-area yards into a searchable interface.
  • Organize vehicles by model, generation and available details.
  • Surface estimated resale opportunities and make browsing practical on a phone.
  • Iterate on pagination, filtering, inventory synchronization and part-fitment information.

Ongoing improvements

Inventory freshness, model matching and resale estimates are evolving features. Estimates are planning aids, not guaranteed resale values.
